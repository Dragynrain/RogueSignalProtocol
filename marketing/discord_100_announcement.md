**Rogue Signal Protocol 1.0 is out.**

The beta is over. Same game, a long polish pass, and a new hands-on tutorial that teaches the mechanics by making them necessary - no popups.

New in 1.0:
- 5-section prologue tutorial (dying in it doesn't cost your save)
- No CMD window on Windows launch anymore
- Atomic saves plus a pile of pathfinding and UI fixes from the pre-release sweep

Still here: 3 network levels, 8 enemy types, 13 exploits, 47 achievements, 20-level Ascension, full gamepad and Steam Deck support, ASCII or graphical tiles.

Runs are 10-15 minutes. Permadeath. The first few will go badly.
