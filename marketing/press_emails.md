# Press and creator emails

Send in this order, one or two a day, Tuesday to Thursday morning. Put the links in the body. Don't attach the zip (34 MB, over Gmail's limit).

Send from roguesignalprotocol@gmail.com so replies land in the press inbox.

1. Alpha Beta Gamer - free games are their whole beat, and they run two YouTube channels plus a Discord. Best reach for the effort.
2. GamingOnLinux - native Linux, open source, Steam Deck. Liam covers itch.io games and keeps itch Linux collections.
3. Free Game Planet - same admin as Alpha Beta Gamer, so wait 3-4 days after email 1 rather than sending both at once.
4. YouTubers (Splattercat, Nookrium, Retromation, RhapsodyPlays) - one at a time, each with a line you write after watching one of their recent videos.
