🧱 1. Itch.io Page: Polished, But Lean

Your page should be:

Clean
Functional
Clearly geared toward gathering feedback

 Must-Haves:

Short game description (2-3 sentences max): Explain what the game is, what genre elements it includes (turn-based, traditional roguelike, cyberpunk, etc.).
One good trailer OR 1-2 GIFs: Real gameplay. No fluff, no cinematic trailer - /r/roguelikedev prefers substance.
Screenshots (2–4 max): Show UI, movement, and one combat example.
Known limitations: List what isn't finished or what you're actively seeking feedback on. Be transparent.
Link to feedback form or how you want to receive feedback.
Instructions: How to play, controls, any hotkeys or important mechanics.

📝 2. Reddit Post on /r/roguelikedev: Honest, Specific, and Engaging

You're not marketing to strangers - you're speaking to peers. Treat the post like a devlog but with a playable prototype.

 Include:

A personal tone: “Hey folks, I’ve been working on this for X months...”
Purpose of the release: “I’m looking for feedback on X systems, especially Y mechanic...”
Link to Itch.io + brief game description.
1–2 GIFs or screenshots inline (Reddit supports image embeds).
A direct call to action: “I’d really love feedback on [combat balance / UI clarity / procedural generation].”
Optional but useful: Mention if you're planning to post a follow-up on what you learned from this alpha.

📋 3. Feedback Form (Highly Recommended)

You will not get good feedback without guiding people. Provide structure to help them help you.

 Recommended Tools:

Google Forms or Typeform (free tier is fine)

📄 What to Include in the Form:
How far did you get?
What confused you the most?
What part was the most fun?
What part felt tedious or frustrating?
Any bugs or crashes?
(Optional) Would you play again? Why or why not?
(Optional) Email or Discord for follow-up

Keep it short - 5-7 questions max. Offer a comment box at the end for general thoughts.

🧰 4. Re-Usable Assets: Keep These Light

These are nice to have for reusing in devlogs, Discord, or future marketing, but don’t overbuild for Alpha:

Asset	Effort Level	Reason

Short gameplay GIFs (2–3)	 Worth it	Reddit & Discord love GIFs

One logo or title screen	👍 Basic branding helps	But don't agonize over design

Tagline or one-liner	👍 Optional	Useful in Reddit post or feedback form
"A cyberspace stealth roguelike where detection means deletion."

Devlog template or blog post	🤏 Optional	/r/roguelikedev loves dev process breakdowns

💬 5. Community Engagement (Do This!)

Respond to every comment on Reddit.

Thank testers.

Consider joining their feedback Discord or inviting them to yours.

If feedback is implemented, follow up later with: “Here’s what changed based on your input…”
