1. Visual Assets (Images, Screenshots, Logos, Icons)

a) Screenshots (6-8 images)

These will primarily be used on your itch.io page, social media posts, and marketing materials. Each screenshot should showcase different aspects of your game.
Action Shot: Combat or key gameplay moment.
Atmosphere Shot: Environmental art showcasing the cyberpunk world.
UI Showcase: A screenshot highlighting the interface or special features (e.g., menus, inventory).
Character/Enemy Art: Show a unique character or enemy from the game.
Roguelike Mechanics: A shot illustrating your game's core mechanics like procedural generation, permadeath, or skill upgrades.
Boss Battle or Special Event: Highlight one of the larger or climactic moments.
Recommended Size:
1080px x 720px (landscape orientation).
Aspect Ratio: 16:9 (standard landscape size).

b) GIFs (3-5 GIFs)

GIFs are excellent for showcasing gameplay in motion. They’re particularly effective on platforms like Twitter, Reddit, Instagram, and even itch.io devlogs.
Combat Example: A few seconds showing the turn-based battle system in action.
Exploration/Movement: A clip showing a player navigating the environment or a unique game mechanic in action.
Ability/Skill Showcase: Demonstrate a special attack, power-up, or the impact of a roguelike feature (e.g., random loot drop).
Recommended Size:
600px x 400px
Max 8 MB size for quicker loading (Keep it optimized but still visually appealing).

c) Game Logo (1-2 logos)

Main Logo: The primary logo for your game. This should be visually striking and give a strong sense of the cyberpunk aesthetic.
Simplified Icon: A smaller version of the logo or a symbol/icon for use on social media profile pictures, buttons, or small banners.
Recommended Size:
Main Logo: 1000px x 1000px
Icon Version: 500px x 500px

  b) Long Game Description

  Rogue Signal Protocol is a cyberspace stealth roguelike where you play as a digitized consciousness trapped inside a hostile corporate network. Your memories are fragmented. The security systems
  are hunting something that shouldn't exist: you. Strange echoes linger in the data streams—traces of others who came before. The network whispers of failures, experiments, and something called
  a resilience initiative. But the only test that matters now is survival.

  Infiltrate three increasingly hostile networks—Corporate, Government, and Military—each filled with sophisticated AI security systems designed to detect and eliminate unauthorized
  consciousnesses. Hide in digital shadows, deploy 12 powerful hacking exploits ranging from stealth tools to devastating cyberattacks, and discover 20+ narrative fragments that slowly reveal
  what happened to you. Every action generates trace data that alerts enemies to your presence. Overuse your abilities and you'll overheat, causing CPU damage that brings you closer to total
  system failure. And when you die? Your save file is deleted. Forever. Become the rogue signal that shatters the firewalls, or fade into the network's ghost data like the others before you.

  - Enemy Movement Prediction: See enemies' next 3 planned moves for tactical advantage—slip past patrols or set up ambushes
  - Stealth-First Infiltration: Hide in blind spots, manage your trace signature, and avoid detection—combat is dangerous, silence is survival
  - Achievement System: Persistent tracking across runs with unlockable challenges proving your mastery
  - Interactive Look Mode: Inspect enemies, items, and terrain with mouse or keyboard for detailed tactical intel
  - 12 Powerful Exploits: From Shadow Step teleportation to System Crash emergency protocols—combine stealth, combat, and utility tools with heat management
  - 8 Unique Enemy Types: Face surveillance Scanners, deadly Admin Avatars, corrupting Viruses, and adaptive AI hunters
  - Dual Rendering Modes: Classic ASCII/Unicode or full graphical sprites—same gameplay, your choice of aesthetic
  - Full Audio Design: Atmospheric music and 40+ sound effects (toggleable) enhance the cyberspace immersion
  - Permadeath with Stakes: Death deletes your save file. Auto-save after every action. No second chances. Every decision carries weight
  - Procedural Generation: Every run creates new network layouts, enemy placements, and resource distributions

  About Rogue Signal Protocol

  Development Background

  Rogue Signal Protocol was built using Python and the TCOD library, combining classic roguelike mechanics with modern stealth gameplay. The game features procedurally generated levels,
  permadeath consequences, and a mystery-driven narrative inspired by cyberpunk classics like System Shock and Deus Ex.

  "I wanted to create a stealth roguelike where the setting justified the mechanics," says developer Adam Forster. "Your consciousness is literally trapped in a digital prison. The shadows are
  data obfuscation. Heat is processing load. Every system reinforces the fiction that you're a rogue signal trying to escape deletion."

  Q: How long does a full run take?A: A complete run through all three networks takes 10-15 minutes depending on playstyle. Most deaths happen much faster—this is a challenging game!

  Q: Is permadeath really permanent?A: Yes. When you die, your save file is deleted automatically. This is a core theme—your digital consciousness is being erased. No backups, no restores.

  Q: What makes this different from other stealth roguelikes?A: Four unique elements: (1) enemies show their planned movement 3 turns ahead for tactical planning, (2) achievement system
