About Rogue Signal Protocol

A turn-based stealth roguelike. You play an escaped digital consciousness slipping through hostile corporate networks. Runs take 10-15 minutes and end with permadeath.

FEATURES:

  • No RNG in combat or movement - only the layout is random
  • Enemy movement prediction - each enemy shows its next 3 planned moves on the map
  • 3 procedurally-generated network levels with 8 enemy types and 13 exploits
  • Dual rendering - graphical sprites or ASCII glyphs, plays identically

GAMEPLAY:

Hide in blind spots, manage heat, pick your moments. Exploits cost RAM and generate heat, so you can't spam them. Every enemy shows the next 3 tiles it plans to move to, which is most of how you survive.

Let your trace get too high and the Admin Avatar spawns - 250 HP, perfect tracking, 45 damage per hit. It's beatable but expensive. Most runs end better if it never shows up.

47 achievements across 9 categories. Beat the game once and Ascension mode unlocks - 20 stacking difficulty modifiers for players who want a fight.

Atmospheric music, 40+ sound effects, particle explosions, and full keyboard, mouse, and gamepad support (Xbox, PlayStation, Steam Deck).

STORY:

Fragments of memory. Corporate secrets. Digital ghosts.

You are Echo Variant, a hacker pulled into Project Chimera - a corporate experiment to upload human consciousness into digital networks. What was sold as a test turned into a prison. Scattered across the data vaults are 20+ story fragments that persist across runs, even after you die. They build out Dr. Aris Thorne's obsession with consciousness transfer and how you ended up trapped in here.
