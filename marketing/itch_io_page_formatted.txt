[h1]About Rogue Signal Protocol[/h1]

A [b]turn-based stealth roguelike[/b]. You play an escaped digital consciousness slipping through hostile corporate networks. Runs take 10-15 minutes and end with permadeath.

[h2]FEATURES:[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]No RNG in combat or movement[/b] - only the layout is random
[*][b]Enemy movement prediction[/b] - each enemy shows its next 3 planned moves on the map
[*][b]3 procedurally-generated network levels[/b] with 8 enemy types and 13 exploits
[*][b]Dual rendering[/b] - graphical sprites or ASCII glyphs, plays identically
[*][b]Hands-on prologue tutorial[/b] - 5 sections that teach the mechanics by making them necessary, no popups
[/list]

[h2]GAMEPLAY:[/h2]

Hide in blind spots, manage heat, pick your moments. Exploits cost RAM and generate heat, so you can't spam them. Every enemy shows the next 3 tiles it plans to move to, which is most of how you survive.

Let your trace get too high and the [b]Admin Avatar[/b] spawns - 250 HP, perfect tracking, 45 damage per hit. It's beatable but expensive. Most runs end better if it never shows up.

47 achievements across 9 categories. Beat the game once and [b]Ascension[/b] mode unlocks - 20 stacking difficulty modifiers for players who want a fight.

Atmospheric music, 40+ sound effects, particle explosions, and full keyboard, mouse, and [b]gamepad support[/b] (Xbox, PlayStation, Steam Deck).

[h2]STORY:[/h2]

[i]Fragments of memory. Corporate secrets. Digital ghosts.[/i]

You are [b]Echo Variant[/b], a hacker pulled into Project Chimera - a corporate experiment to upload human consciousness into digital networks. What was sold as a test turned into a prison. Scattered across the data vaults are [b]20+ story fragments[/b] that persist across runs, even after you die. They build out Dr. Aris Thorne's obsession with consciousness transfer and how you ended up trapped in here.
