# Ascension System

Post-game difficulty scaling for experienced players.

## Overview

Ascension is unlocked after completing the game. Each Ascension level adds a new modifier that stacks on top of all previous ones. At Ascension 20, you're dealing with all 20 modifiers at once.

**Access:** Press **N** during gameplay to view and adjust your Ascension level.

**Persistence:** Your Ascension level is saved and persists across runs.

## Ascension Modifiers

Each level adds one new modifier. All previous modifiers remain active.

| Level | Modifier | Effect |
|-------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Scanner Vision | Scanners gain +1 vision range |
| 2 | Enemy Durability | All enemies gain +10 HP |
| 3 | Trace Amplification | Trace gain multiplied by 2x |
| 4 | Enemy Damage | Enemy damage multiplied by 1.2x |
| 5 | Enemy Awareness | All enemies gain +1 vision range |
| 6 | Reduced Cover | 1 fewer blind spot per floor |
| 7 | Hostile Trace | +20% trace gain from hostile actions |
| 8 | Heat Persistence | Heat reduction capped at 1 per turn |
| 9 | Reinforcements | +5 enemies per floor |
| 10 | Tunnel Vision | Player vision capped at 12 |
| 11 | Code Scarcity | 2 fewer data codes per floor (minimum 3) |
| 12 | Elite Spawns | Nastier enemy spawn weights |
| 13 | Node Rationing | Reduced node capacity per floor |
| 14 | RAM Shortage | Start with only 6 RAM |
| 15 | Alert Propagation | Alerts spread to range 10 |
| 16 | Cramped Quarters | Smaller rooms, fewer hiding spots |
| 17 | Melee Penalty | Melee attacks generate +5 heat |
| 18 | Upgrade Drought | 1 fewer upgrade per floor |
| 19 | Node Shortage | 1 fewer data node per floor |
| 20 | Fragile Cover | Blind spots become single-use |

## Difficulty Curve

### Early Ascension (1-5)
Enemies become stronger and more aware. You can still play aggressively with good positioning.

### Mid Ascension (6-10)
Resources start shrinking. Trace management becomes critical. Combat gets riskier.

### High Ascension (11-15)
Every resource is scarce. Stealth is no longer optional. Mistakes compound quickly.

### Extreme Ascension (16-20)
The map itself works against you. Level 20 removes the safety of blind spots entirely - they disappear after one use.

## Achievements

Four achievements track Ascension progress:

- **Sensor Sweep** - Complete Ascension 5
- **Firewall Breaker** - Complete Ascension 10
- **Silent Running** - Complete Ascension 15
- **Ascension Master** - Complete Ascension 20

## Tips

- Master the base game before attempting Ascension
- Each modifier demands a different adjustment to your playstyle
- Stealth builds scale better into high Ascension than combat builds
- Learn which modifiers counter your usual strategies and adapt
- The modifiers stack - by A10 you're playing a different game
