# Achievement Guide

Complete guide to all achievements in Rogue Signal Protocol.

## Overview

There are **26 achievements** organized into 6 categories. Some achievements are **hidden** until unlocked to preserve surprise and challenge.

**Progress Tracking:**
- Achievements unlock in real-time during gameplay
- Progress persists across all runs (even after death)
- View unlocked achievements by pressing **V** key in-game
- Popup notifications appear when achievements unlock (can be toggled in settings)

---

## Achievement Categories

### 🗡️ Combat Mastery (5 Achievements)

Master the art of digital combat and enemy elimination.

#### ⚔️ First Blood
**Kill your first enemy**
- **Unlock Condition:** Kill any enemy
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Tips:** Unavoidable in most runs - combat is often necessary

#### 💀 Massacre
**Kill 20+ enemies in one run**
- **Unlock Condition:** Eliminate 20 or more enemies across all 3 levels in single run
- **Difficulty:** Medium
- **Tips:** Go loud on Corporate Network (19 enemies), continue aggressive play through Government System

#### 💥 Overkill
**Deal 50+ damage in a single hit**
- **Unlock Condition:** One attack deals 50 or more damage
- **Difficulty:** Medium-Hard
- **Tips:**
  - Buffer Overflow (40 damage) + Blind Spot Bonus (+10) = 50 damage!
  - Ambush from blind spots for guaranteed unlock
  - Works with any high-damage exploit + bonuses

#### 🌀 Crowd Control
**Hit 5+ enemies with one AOE exploit**
- **Unlock Condition:** Single area-of-effect attack hits 5 or more enemies
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Tips:**
  - Use Logic Bomb (radius 2) or Denial of Service (radius 1)
  - Wait for enemies to cluster near chokepoints
  - System Crash (radius 3) makes this easier if you can survive self-damage
  - Military Backbone has most enemies (38 total)

#### 🎯 Efficient Killer
**Average 2+ kills per turn for 10+ turns**
- **Unlock Condition:** Kill 20+ enemies in 10 consecutive turns
- **Difficulty:** Very Hard
- **Tips:**
  - Requires aggressive, optimized combat
  - Use AOE exploits to hit multiple targets
  - Plan ahead to ensure enemies are always in range
  - Best attempted on Military Backbone with dense enemy spawns

### 🕵️ Stealth Mastery (4 Achievements)

Demonstrate perfect infiltration without detection.

#### 🔪 Silent Assassin
**Kill 10 enemies without being detected**
- **Unlock Condition:** Kill 10 enemies while they're Unaware (yellow)
- **Difficulty:** Medium
- **Tips:**
  - Attack from blind spots to stay hidden longer
  - Use Traffic Masquerade (invisibility) to position for ambushes
  - Killing alerts nearby enemies, so space out eliminations
  - Track undetected kills in achievement progress

#### 👻 Ghost Protocol
**Complete a level without being detected**
- **Unlock Condition:** Reach gateway without any enemy entering Alert (orange) or Hostile (red) state
- **Difficulty:** Medium-Hard
- **Tips:**
  - Easiest on Corporate Network (fewest enemies, most blind spots)
  - Use System Hop to teleport past enemy vision cones
  - Traffic Masquerade allows walking past enemies
  - Can still kill enemies if done from blind spots without triggering detection

#### 🌑 Blind Spot Master
**Kill 5+ enemies from blind spots in one run**
- **Unlock Condition:** Kill 5 enemies while standing in blind spots (.) or ghost nodes
- **Difficulty:** Easy-Medium
- **Tips:**
  - Blind spots grant +10 damage bonus
  - Use Threat Scan to see enemy vision ranges
  - Position in blind spots, wait for enemies to approach
  - Combine with Silent Assassin for double achievement progress

#### 🕶️ Invisible Victory
**Win the game without ever being detected**
- **Unlock Condition:** Complete all 3 levels without enemies entering Alert or Hostile states
- **Difficulty:** Very Hard
- **Tips:**
  - Ultimate stealth challenge requiring perfect play
  - Avoid all combat unless from blind spots with 100% safety
  - Traffic Masquerade is essential for Level 3
  - System Hop for emergency escapes
  - Trace management critical - use Log Wiper and Ghost Nodes
  - Practice Ghost Protocol on individual levels first

### Efficiency & Speed (4 Achievements)

Complete runs with speed and resource management mastery.

#### Speedrunner
**Win in under 100 turns**
- **Unlock Condition:** Complete all 3 levels in 99 turns or fewer
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Tips:**
  - Minimize exploration - beeline to gateway
  - Skip optional combat and pickups
  - Use System Hop for rapid traversal
  - Memorize efficient paths through level types
  - Practice individual level speedruns first

#### 🔥 Heat Master
**Win while staying under 50 heat**
- **Unlock Condition:** Complete game with max heat never exceeding 49
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Tips:**
  - Avoid heat-heavy exploits (System Crash 35, Denial of Service 35, Logic Bomb 35)
  - Use cooling nodes liberally (6 on Level 1, 4 on Level 2, 2 on Level 3)
  - Let passive cooling work (2/turn, 3/turn with buff)
  - Prioritize stealth over combat
  - Threat Scan (25) and Network Scan (20) are your friends

#### 📦 Resource Efficient
**Win without using any code hacks**
- **Unlock Condition:** Complete all 3 levels without activating any code hack pickups (!)
- **Difficulty:** Medium-Hard
- **Tips:**
  - Simply avoid picking up `!` symbols
  - Use cooling/CPU/ghost nodes instead for resources
  - Collect permanent upgrades - they don't count against this
  - Exploit pickups (&) are allowed
  - More challenging than it sounds - those buffs are tempting!

#### 🧠 Pure Skill **[HIDDEN]**
**Win without using exploits or code hacks**
- **Unlock Condition:** Complete game with 0 exploits equipped and 0 code hacks used
- **Difficulty:** Extreme
- **Tips:**
  - Ultimate challenge - movement and positioning only
  - Cannot equip any exploits (not even utility ones!)
  - Must avoid all combat - 100% stealth required
  - Traffic Masquerade unavailable - rely on blind spots only
  - Extremely difficult, requires perfect level knowledge
  - **Hidden achievement** - doesn't appear until unlocked

### 🏆 Challenge Runs (4 Achievements)

Special challenge conditions for advanced players.

#### 🛡️ Untouchable
**Win without taking any damage**
- **Unlock Condition:** Complete all 3 levels with 0 damage taken
- **Difficulty:** Very Hard
- **Tips:**
  - Avoid all enemy contact
  - Don't use System Crash (deals self-damage)
  - Avoid Logic Bomb friendly fire
  - Pure stealth approach with occasional safe ambushes
  - One mistake ends the run for this achievement
  - CPU restoration doesn't matter - just never take damage

#### 🔍 No Trace
**Win without trace level exceeding 50%**
- **Unlock Condition:** Complete game with max trace never above 50%
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Tips:**
  - Use Ghost Nodes (-20% trace each) aggressively
  - Log Wiper exploit (-30% trace) is essential
  - Avoid detection to minimize trace gain
  - Trace increases 1% per 25 turns passively
  - Reduces by 50% when advancing levels
  - Prevents Admin Avatar spawn on Level 3

#### ✂️ Minimalist
**Win with only 3 or fewer exploits equipped**
- **Unlock Condition:** Complete game with max 3 exploits in equipped slots
- **Difficulty:** Medium-Hard
- **Tips:**
  - Choose versatile exploits: System Hop (escape), Code Injection (damage), Log Wiper (trace)
  - Don't equip more than 3 even if you have extra RAM
  - Can collect exploits, just don't equip them
  - Forces creative problem-solving with limited tools
  - Loadout example: System Hop + Code Injection + Traffic Masquerade

#### ☮️ Pacifist
**Complete a level killing 5 or fewer enemies**
- **Unlock Condition:** Finish any single level with 5 or fewer kills
- **Difficulty:** Medium
- **Tips:**
  - Easiest on Corporate Network (19 enemies, most blind spots)
  - Use System Hop and Traffic Masquerade to avoid combat
  - Kill only when absolutely necessary for path clearing
  - Doesn't require full game completion - just one level
  - Good practice for Invisible Victory achievement

### 💼 Mastery & Collection (4 Achievements)

Demonstrate comprehensive knowledge and usage of game systems.

#### 💻 Master Hacker
**Use all 12 exploits in one run**
- **Unlock Condition:** Activate each of the 12 exploits at least once in single playthrough
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Tips:**
  - **All 12 Exploits:** System Hop, Traffic Masquerade, Decoy Swarm, Memory Leak, Buffer Overflow, Code Injection, Logic Bomb, Denial of Service, Threat Scan, Network Scan, Log Wiper, Antivirus, System Crash
  - Requires significant RAM (32 total RAM = 8 base + 6 upgrades × 4)
  - Collect all exploit pickups (4 on L1, 3 on L2, 2 on L3 = 9 total)
  - Must have 3 exploits pre-unlocked or find them all
  - Swap exploits in/out to activate each one
  - Track which you've used - game doesn't show progress during run

#### 📚 Code Collector
**Use all 6 code hack types in one run**
- **Unlock Condition:** Activate all 6 code hack effect types in single playthrough
- **Difficulty:** Medium
- **Tips:**
  - **All 6 Types:** restore_cpu, reduce_heat, reduce_trace_level, speed_boost, enhanced_vision, exploit_efficiency
  - Code hacks are color-randomized each run
  - Same color = same effect throughout session
  - 12 code hacks on Level 1, 10 on Level 2, 5 on Level 3 = 27 total
  - Discovery tracking shows which types found
  - Easier than Master Hacker - plenty available

#### 📖 Enemy Database
**Encounter all enemy types in one run**
- **Unlock Condition:** See all 8 enemy types during single playthrough
- **Difficulty:** Medium-Easy
- **Tips:**
  - **All 8 Enemy Types:** Scanner (S), Patrol (P), Bot (B), Firewall (F), Hunter (H), Virus (V), Inhibitor (I), Admin Avatar (A)
  - Admin Avatar only spawns at high trace levels on Military Backbone
  - To guarantee: Let trace exceed 70% on Level 3, spawn Admin Avatar, then escape
  - Or play normally and hope for Admin Avatar spawn
  - Other 7 types appear naturally across levels
  - Just need to see them - doesn't require killing

#### 🗺️ Explorer
**Discover all special node types in one run**
- **Unlock Condition:** Use each special node type at least once in single playthrough
- **Difficulty:** Easy-Medium
- **Tips:**
  - **All 3 Node Types:** Cooling Node (♢), CPU Recovery Node (♡), Ghost Node (♤)
  - Abundant on Level 1 (6 of each), fewer on Levels 2-3
  - Use Network Scan exploit to reveal all nodes instantly
  - Must actually use them, not just see them
  - Easy achievement - nodes essential for survival

### 🏅 Lifetime Achievements (4 Achievements)

Long-term progression across multiple playthroughs.

#### 🎖️ Veteran
**Complete 10 games**
- **Unlock Condition:** Finish 10 complete playthroughs (win or lose)
- **Difficulty:** Time-based
- **Tips:**
  - Tracks both victories and deaths
  - Automatic with consistent play
  - 10-15 minutes per run = ~2-3 hours total
  - Progress persists forever

#### 🏆 Persistent
**Win 5 games**
- **Unlock Condition:** Complete successful exfiltrations on 5 separate runs
- **Difficulty:** Medium (skill-based)
- **Tips:**
  - Must win, not just complete
  - Practice makes perfect
  - Each win counts toward Veteran too
  - Master one strategy, then experiment

#### 👑 Legendary **[HIDDEN]**
**Win 20 games**
- **Unlock Condition:** Achieve 20 successful exfiltrations
- **Difficulty:** Hard (time + skill)
- **Tips:**
  - Long-term goal for dedicated players
  - Shows true mastery of game systems
  - ~3-5 hours of successful play
  - **Hidden achievement** - appears after unlocking Persistent
  - Prestige status

#### ⏱️ Survivor
**Survive 500+ turns in a single run**
- **Unlock Condition:** Single playthrough lasts 500 or more turns
- **Difficulty:** Medium-Hard
- **Tips:**
  - Opposite of Speedrunner - take your time
  - Explore every tile, clear every enemy
  - Wait in safe spots to run up turn counter
  - Resource management critical over long run
  - Typical win is 150-250 turns, so this requires deliberate pacing
  - Can win and get Survivor simultaneously

## Achievement Hunting Strategies

### Efficient Unlocking Path

**Beginner Route (First 5 Runs):**
1. First Blood → Automatic in first combat
2. Explorer → Use all 3 node types
3. Enemy Database → See all enemy types
4. Code Collector → Use all 6 code hack types
5. Veteran → Complete 10 games (ongoing)

**Intermediate Route (Skill Building):**
6. Ghost Protocol → Complete one level undetected
7. Pacifist → Complete level with ≤5 kills
8. Blind Spot Master → 5 kills from blind spots
9. Silent Assassin → 10 undetected kills
10. Minimalist → Win with ≤3 exploits

**Advanced Route (Challenge Runs):**
11. No Trace → Win without trace >50%
12. Heat Master → Win without heat >50
13. Untouchable → Win without taking damage
14. Speedrunner → Win in <100 turns
15. Resource Efficient → Win without code hacks

**Expert Route (Mastery):**
16. Master Hacker → Use all 12 exploits
17. Massacre → Kill 20+ enemies
18. Invisible Victory → Win never detected
19. Pure Skill → Win without exploits/code hacks (extreme)

**Lifetime Achievements (Ongoing):**
20. Veteran → 10 completions
21. Persistent → 5 wins
22. Survivor → 500+ turn run
23. Legendary → 20 wins

### Complementary Achievements

Some achievements work well together:
- **Silent Assassin + Blind Spot Master** - Kill from blind spots while undetected
- **Ghost Protocol + Pacifist** - No detection + minimal kills
- **Heat Master + Resource Efficient** - Low resource usage run
- **Speedrunner + Minimalist** - Fast run with limited tools
- **Untouchable + Invisible Victory** - Perfect stealth, no damage

### Conflicting Achievements

Some achievements are mutually exclusive in single run:
- **Speedrunner vs Survivor** - Can't be both fast and slow
- **Massacre vs Pacifist** - Can't kill many and kill few
- **Pure Skill vs Master Hacker** - Can't use all exploits and no exploits
- **Resource Efficient vs Code Collector** - Can't use no code hacks and all code hacks

## Hidden Achievements

Two achievements are hidden until unlocked:

1. **🧠 Pure Skill** - Extreme challenge, meant to be discovered
2. **👑 Legendary** - Long-term goal, revealed after Persistent

Hidden achievements don't show in menu until unlocked, preserving sense of discovery.

---

## Achievement Popups

When achievements unlock:
- Popup appears at top of screen
- Shows achievement name, description, icon
- Fades after 3 seconds
- Can be toggled off in Settings menu
- Doesn't pause gameplay

---

## Progress Tracking

**View Achievements:**
- Press **V** key anytime in game
- Shows all unlocked achievements
- Hidden achievements appear as "???" until unlocked
- Organized by category
- Scroll with arrow keys or mouse wheel

**Progress Persistence:**
- Achievements saved to `saves/progress.json`
- Survives death (not affected by permadeath)
- Survives game uninstall if file backed up
- Lifetime metrics accumulate forever

---

## Tips for Achievement Hunters

1. **Plan your runs** - Some achievements require specific playstyles
2. **Practice individual levels** - Master one level at a time for challenges
3. **Use Network Scan** - Reveals nodes for Explorer achievement
4. **Track your progress** - Keep mental notes on which achievements you're close to
5. **Don't force it** - Many achievements unlock naturally through varied play
6. **Save difficult ones for later** - Pure Skill and Invisible Victory require mastery
7. **Combine achievements** - Try for 2-3 compatible achievements per run
8. **Learn enemy patterns** - Essential for stealth and combat achievements
9. **Master exploits** - Know what each does for Master Hacker
10. **Enjoy the journey** - Achievements add replay value, not stress

Good luck, and may your signals stay rogue!
